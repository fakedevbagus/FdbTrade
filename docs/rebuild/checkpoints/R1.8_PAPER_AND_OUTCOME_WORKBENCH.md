## Outcome

R1.8 adds a private, projection-only paper and outcome workbench over the R1.7
API. It displays the active authoritative signal, verified R1.6 resolution and
lineage, current durable risk state, registered cost assumptions, explicit
quantity and confirmation controls, and durable risk/order/fill/position/
reconciliation/outcome facts.

## Observable behavior

- Opening, changing, refreshing or reopening the workbench performs GET only.
- Confirmation requires an explicit acknowledgement and emits exactly one
  strict R1.7 payload. A client in-flight lock plus R1.7 idempotency prevents
  double-submit duplication.
- R1.6 baseline costs are clearly labeled assumptions and not provider
  observations; conversion direction and immutable dataset/bar lineage are
  displayed rather than recomputed.
- Kill and red risk states are prominent. Pending, running, succeeded, blocked,
  rejected, failed, loading, empty and unavailable states remain distinct.
- Durable list/detail GETs verify R1.6 evidence and R0.9 recovery and
  reconciliation. Refresh/restart reopens the run from SQLite.
- Malformed or unknown responses fail closed. No authoritative result is stored
  or invented in browser persistence.
