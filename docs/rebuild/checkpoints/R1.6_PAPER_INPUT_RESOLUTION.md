## Outcome

R1.6 adds a deterministic SQLite authority for cost and USD-conversion inputs
that R0.9 must never trust from a UI. R0.9 now accepts only a verified
resolution identity plus quantity and no longer accepts caller-selected spread,
slippage or conversion provenance.

## Observable authority behavior

- A frozen registered baseline supplies 0.6 pip spread and 0.1 pip slippage for
  all seven pairs, explicitly marked as assumptions rather than provider data.
- EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD and NZDUSD use quote-USD identity conversion. USDJPY,
  USDCHF and USDCAD invert the exact event-time execution bar. No implicit
  third-currency cross is used.
- Every resolution pins signal/execution dataset identities and artifact
  digests, event time, assessment time, conversion bar digest and config digest.
- Both datasets must be accepted, match the signal instrument/timeframe and
  contain exactly one event-time bar. Inputs older than two bars fail closed.
- Missing, stale, ambiguous, mismatched, corrupt or config-drifted evidence is
  persisted as an explicit blocked resolution run.
- Duplicate requests replay the same identity. Restart resets interrupted work
  and verifies terminal resolution bytes against current immutable inputs.
- R0.9 re-verifies the resolution before its unchanged durable risk decision,
  paper ledger, reconciliation and outcome flow.

## Preserved boundaries

- Mandatory persisted approved risk before paper remains enforced.
- R0.7 signal rules and the R0.8/R1.4/R1.5 research meanings are unchanged.
- No route/UI, automatic execution, provider/network access, scheduling,
  demo/live/provider-order transport, research promotion, legacy-backtest
  rewire or M48 authority was added.
- The ledger now contains twelve ordered reversible migrations through
  `0012_paper_input_resolution`.
- All ten M48 files remain byte-identical: 55,941 bytes and aggregate manifest
  digest `f7dc5aacade5078cf934be760651c2e59d9e340c7a096a25629006d8f8555fe6`.
