## Outcome

R0.9 makes SQLite authoritative for the mandatory pre-paper risk verdict,
latched risk state, append-only paper order/fill/position ledger,
reconciliation and operational paper outcomes. Migration
`0008_risk_paper_outcomes_authority` adds eleven authority tables plus
immutability and cross-boundary triggers.

## Mandatory risk and kill latch

Every paper order is bound one-to-one to an R0.7 signal candidate and persisted
risk decision. The authority reopens and re-hashes the candidate's R0.6 source
dataset, rejects expired/non-authoritative candidates, reconstructs account and
open-position state from the SQLite fill ledger and pins the risk-state event
used by the decision. SQL triggers reject submit, acknowledgement, fill and
position events without an approved decision.

Risk state is a chronological append-only event stream. `kill` never resets
automatically. Explicit operator release lands in `red`; a second explicit
state override is required before new entries can be approved. Rejected
decisions produce durable order-created/risk-checked/rejected evidence and no
submission or fill.

## Paper ledger and reconciliation

Approved orders use the existing deterministic realistic paper policy over a
verified matching R0.6 closed-bar cascade. Orders, lifecycle events, fills and
opened/closed position snapshots are immutable and append-only. Each terminal
run replays the full ledger and event log; a discrepancy prevents terminal
publication rather than being repaired or ignored.

The implementation is deliberately local paper simulation. It has no network
client, provider adapter, provider-order submission or live execution path.

## Outcomes and interpretation

A closed paper position creates one immutable outcome linking signal snapshot,
risk decision, order and position. Attribution reports cover the durable paper
outcome set and reconcile to realized PnL. Outcome evidence records paper-only
operation, null uncalibrated signal confidence, no promotion eligibility and
no use of R0.8 backtest evidence as confidence.

## Recovery and idempotency

Interrupted running work returns to pending. Recovery verifies decision,
order, fill and outcome digests, deterministically replays the persisted risk
request and reconciles the complete ledger. Fault tests cover interruption
after the risk decision and a lost response after terminal commit. Replaying
the same request returns the existing result; a divergent request for the same
candidate fails closed.
