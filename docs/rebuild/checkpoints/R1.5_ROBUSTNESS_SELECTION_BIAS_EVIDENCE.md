## Outcome

R1.5 adds a separate SQLite and immutable-artifact authority over successful
R1.4 temporal evidence. It atomically predeclares a frozen six-trial cost grid,
evaluates every chronological-test and rolling-test slice, records sensitivity,
sample, regime and timeframe evidence, then emits exactly `pass`,
`insufficient-evidence` or `rejected` with limitations.

## Observable authority behavior

- The experiment identity accepts only one intact successful R1.4 run and the
  frozen R1.5 configuration. Duplicate requests reopen the same identity.
- The ledger contains baseline, spread-mid, spread-adverse, slippage-mid,
  slippage-adverse and combined-adverse declarations before execution. A
  terminal result requires all six in their declared order.
- Spread sensitivity is bounded to 0.6/1.2/2.4 pips and slippage sensitivity to
  0.1/0.3/0.6 pips. No arbitrary parameter surface is exposed.
- Minimum evidence is 120 bars, three walk-forward folds, three baseline closed
  trades and one known-regime closed trade. Sparse evidence is terminal
  `insufficient-evidence`; thresholds are never relaxed.
- Sufficient evidence is rejected for non-positive baseline/stressed return,
  adverse-cost retention below 50%, unstable cost sensitivity or drawdown over
  25%. Passing evidence still carries historical, single-dataset/timeframe and
  cost-grid limitations.
- Timeframe evidence names the exact dataset timeframe. Regime evidence records
  closed-trade count and realized PnL for trend, range, high-volatility,
  low-volatility, transition and unknown buckets.
- Restart resumes the same ledger. Completed trials are immutable and must
  reproduce byte-for-byte; R1.4 input, R0.6 dataset and R1.5 output tampering
  fail closed.
