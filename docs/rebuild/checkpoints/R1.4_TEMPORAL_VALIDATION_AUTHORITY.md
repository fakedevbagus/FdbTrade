## Outcome

R1.4 adds a separate SQLite and immutable-artifact authority for chronological
train/validation/test and rolling walk-forward evaluation of the frozen R0.8
deterministic baseline. It pins the source dataset, exact evaluated slices,
baseline and temporal configuration, split plans, embargo boundaries, realistic
costs, deterministic seed, engine results, metrics, manifests, and summaries.

## Observable authority behavior

- The chronological plan is fixed at 50/25/25 with two-bar gaps and explicit
  index plus UTC boundaries for train, validation, test, and both embargoes.
- Rolling walk-forward uses fixed 40-bar train, two-bar gap, 12-bar test,
  14-bar step, two-bar post-test embargo, rolling mode, and at least three
  folds. Test windows and their embargoes cannot overlap.
- Every section and fold runs only the frozen R0.8 costs and R0.7 baseline.
  There is no parameter input, fitting, sweep, ranking, or selection surface.
- Rejected-quality data and fewer than 82 bars produce durable `blocked`
  evidence. Overlap, reverse chronology, wrong gap, future leakage, and embargo
  violations are rejected before evaluation.
- Duplicate dataset/config requests reopen one durable identity. Restart
  recovery resumes interrupted work and verifies predecessor registration,
  R0.6 input bytes, R1.4 output bytes, split/config/cost/seed lineage, results,
  metrics, manifests, and summary digests.
- SQLite tables and terminal records are immutable; artifact publication is
  fsync-before-rename under a private staging directory.
